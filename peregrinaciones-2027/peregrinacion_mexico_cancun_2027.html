What is the pilgrimage to Mexico, Cholula, Puebla and Cancún?

The Grand Jubilee Year Pilgrimage to Our Lady of Guadalupe is a Catholic spiritual journey lasting 10 days and 9 nights, on June 4–13, 2027, through Mexico City, Teotihuacán, Cholula, Puebla and Cancún. You will visit the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe, the Shrine of Our Lady of Remedies and Mary Undoer of Knots on a journey of faith, prayer and encounter with Our Lady, surrounded by the history of Mexico and the beauty of the Caribbean.

Trip Details

Mexico and Our Lady of Guadalupe

An encounter with Our Lady of Guadalupe invites us to place our intentions in her hands and renew our hope. The Basilica is a place for prayer, gratitude and encounter with Mary.

The Zócalo, Bellas Artes and Paseo de la Reforma evoke the heart of Mexico through expressions of art, history and tradition.

Devotion to Our Lady of Guadalupe brings generations together around Mary and Saint Juan Diego. Her message of love and comfort continues to inspire those who approach her with faith.

Mexico City brings together an ancient heritage and a deep religious tradition expressed in its squares, churches and monuments.

Puebla and Cholula

Among colorful facades, decorative tiles and towers rising toward heaven, Puebla invites you to contemplate beauty and find moments of quiet reflection.

In Cholula, the Shrine of Our Lady of Remedies and San Gabriel Convent evoke a faith that accompanies the lives and traditions of its people.

Puebla and Cholula bring together Indigenous heritage and the religious art of the colonial era. Their streets and churches preserve the memory of that encounter.

Devotion to Our Lady of Remedies is part of the identity of Cholula, where history and spirituality intertwine.

Cancún and the Caribbean

The white sand and turquoise shades of the Caribbean invite you to pause, give thanks for your experiences and find peace by the sea.

Devotion to Mary Undoer of Knots invites us to entrust our difficulties to Our Lady and open our hearts to hope.

The Mexican Caribbean evokes the heritage of Maya culture and a close relationship with the sea. In its landscapes, nature accompanies encounters with the traditions of the region.

Prayer to Mary Undoer of Knots expresses trust in her intercession amid the worries and challenges of everyday life.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the pilgrimage and which destinations will we visit?
The pilgrimage takes place from June 4 to 13, 2027 and lasts 10 days and 9 nights. We will visit Mexico City, Teotihuacán, Puebla, Cholula and Cancún, combining encounters of faith, cultural tours and time to rest.
What are the main visits and spiritual activities?
We will visit the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe, the Shrine of Our Lady of Remedies in Cholula and the Shrine of Mary Undoer of Knots in Cancún. We will also visit San José del Altillo, where the remains of Blessed Concepción Cabrera de Armida rest. The program includes Eucharistic celebrations, adoration and times for retreat and prayer.
Where in the world can I join this 2027 pilgrimage to Our Lady of Guadalupe from?
From anywhere in the world. We can help you coordinate flights and connections to join the group. Contact us for guidance.
Can I travel on my own and join the group?
Yes, solo travelers are very welcome to join the pilgrimage. Many pilgrims travel on their own. Contact us for details on how to join the group.
Do you organize pilgrimages for groups?
Yes. We organize parish, family and community groups and help with dates, flights and logistics.
