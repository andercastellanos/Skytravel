2026 Marian Pilgrimage: a journey of faith and encounter

Our 2026 Marian Pilgrimage, from September 5 to 16, was much more than a route through sacred places: it was a journey of faith, prayer and fraternity that left a deep mark on the heart of every pilgrim.

The path began in Lisbon and continued to Fatima, where we lived moments of special reflection at the Chapel of the Apparitions, walked the Via Crucis in the Valinhos, and learned about the homes of the shepherd children. Eucharistic celebrations, the rosary and Marian processions renewed our trust in the Virgin Mary.

In Salamanca we discovered some of the city’s most representative places: Plaza Mayor, Casa de las Conchas, the University of Salamanca, the New Cathedral and Old Cathedral, the Convent of San Esteban and the historic Roman Bridge. We also enjoyed beautiful views of the cathedrals and discovered the historical, cultural and spiritual richness of this extraordinary city.

The pilgrimage continued to Covadonga, where we visited its emblematic sanctuary and had the grace to celebrate the Eucharist in an atmosphere of deep reflection, gratitude and communion, surrounded by the extraordinary natural beauty of Asturias.

We also visited Garabandal, a place of deep spirituality nestled among the mountains of Cantabria. There we received the blessing of sharing with Jacinta González, one of the four visionaries of Garabandal. This encounter was one of the most special and emotional moments of our pilgrimage and allowed us to live a close experience of prayer, reflection and faith.

Later we arrived in Lourdes, where we visited the Grotto of Massabielle, its basilicas and the places connected to the life of Saint Bernadette. One of the most moving moments was our participation in the traditional torch procession, during which we walked united in prayer, lighting the night as an expression of faith, hope and devotion to the Virgin Mary.

In Zaragoza we visited the Basilica of Our Lady of the Pillar, where we were privileged to celebrate the Eucharist. This encounter before the Virgin of the Pilar was another moment of deep spirituality, gratitude and renewal of our faith.

Our journey concluded in Madrid, where we had the opportunity to celebrate the Eucharist at the Parish of Our Lady of África. This celebration was the perfect closing to our pilgrimage: a moment to thank God for the path traveled, for the experiences shared and for all the blessings received.

Each place, celebration and encounter strengthened the bonds of the group and reminded us that to pilgrimage is to walk together toward God. We returned home carrying unforgettable memories, great lessons, new friendships and a renewed faith that will remain forever.
