Pilgrimages to Medjugorje and the Holy Land in 2027

Home›Blog›Pilgrimages to Medjugorje and the Holy Land in 2027
Pilgrimages·Pilgrimage Guides·November 3, 2026·By Doris Pérez

Taking part in a pilgrimage is an opportunity to strengthen your faith, share with other believers and dedicate time to prayer. If you are planning your next trip, here you will find answers about dates, prices, included services and payment methods.

At Sky Travel J&M, we organize Catholic pilgrimages in Spanish, with priestly accompaniment and daily celebration of the Eucharist. Discover our options for 2027 and consult our team about the program that best suits your needs.

Are there trips to Medjugorje from the United States?

Yes. At Sky Travel J&M, we organize Spanish-speaking pilgrimages to Medjugorje for people traveling from the United States.

Miami is one of the airports we regularly use for our departures. If you live in Houston, Los Angeles, Chicago or another city, you can request an airfare quote from your place of origin. We can also advise you on connecting to the airport where the group departs.

The price of the land portion of the same program remains the same regardless of your city of origin; airfare varies depending on the route and availability.

If you prefer to purchase your own flights or use miles and points, you can do so by coordinating arrival and departure times with our team in advance.

How much does a pilgrimage to Medjugorje cost?

The price of a pilgrimage to Medjugorje depends on the program selected, the departure city and the type of accommodation.

We offer options starting at €1,999 per person departing from Miami and €2,299 departing from Bogotá. Both rates are quoted in euros and include the land portion and airfare, with double or triple occupancy. Check the price for the program and departure you choose.

If you prefer a single room, you may request one, subject to availability and at an additional cost.

If you are traveling from another city, our team can prepare a quote showing the total price, included services and available flight options.

What is included in the price of a pilgrimage to Medjugorje?

Our program with 7 nights of accommodation at Casa Mirjana includes the following services:

  • ✈️ Airfare.
  • 🏨 Accommodation: 7 nights at Casa Mirjana.
  • 🍽️ Full board: breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • 🎟️ Admission to the sites mentioned in the itinerary.
  • 🧭 A Spanish-speaking Catholic guide throughout the trip.
  • 🚌 Bus transportation according to the itinerary.
  • ✝️ Spiritual accompaniment by a priest.
  • 🙏 Participation in the parish program: praying the rosary, celebration of Holy Mass and adoration, according to the itinerary.

What is not included?

  • 🥤 Beverages with meals.
  • 🩺 Medical insurance.
  • 💵 Tips.
  • 🛍️ Personal and additional expenses.
  • 📋 Any service not mentioned in the inclusions section.

Tips of USD 10 per person per day are recommended for the driver, guide, restaurant staff, porters and other services.

What is the best time to visit Medjugorje?

For our pilgrimages, we especially recommend spring and fall. We offer departures in March and April, May, and September and October 2027.

Your choice also depends on your availability and the program you wish to join. Our team can help you identify the departure that best fits your dates.

How much does a pilgrimage to the Holy Land from the United States cost?

To find out the price of a pilgrimage to the Holy Land from the United States, you need to consult the program and request a quote for flights from your city of origin.

We can review options from Miami and other cities, distinguishing the price of the land portion from the cost of airfare. Request a quote to find out the total price and the services included in the program you choose.

What are the most famous pilgrimages?

Among the best-known Catholic pilgrimage destinations are the Holy Land, Rome and the Vatican, Fátima, Lourdes, Santiago de Compostela and the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe. Medjugorje also welcomes many pilgrims seeking a time of prayer and spiritual encounter.

Each destination offers a different experience. When choosing, you can consider your devotion, the places you wish to visit, the time available and the spiritual accompaniment offered by the program.

Can I participate on my own or with my parish?

Yes. You can travel on your own and join one of our groups, or request a proposal to travel with your parish or community.

If you would like to invite your parish priest, our team can prepare a proposal for the group and coordinate the details of the pilgrimage.

Do the pilgrimages offer payment plans?

Yes. At Sky Travel J&M, we offer payment plans and accept different payment methods, including credit cards.

We offer installments starting at USD 150 per month on selected programs, subject to the conditions of each plan. Consult our team about installment amounts, payment currency and deadlines for the program you choose.
