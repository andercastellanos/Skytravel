Beginner's Guide to Your First Catholic Pilgrimage

Recommendations for living the journey with faith, peace, and an open heart

Home›Blog›First Catholic Pilgrimage
Pilgrimages·Pilgrimage Guides·September 18, 2026·By Doris Pérez
Deciding to go on a Catholic pilgrimage for the first time can bring great excitement, but also questions: Will I be prepared? Will I fit in with the group? How should I prepare spiritually, and what can I expect from the experience? You do not need previous experience or advanced religious training. What matters most is beginning with an open attitude, a willingness to share, and a desire to draw closer to God.Sky Travel J&M

These concerns are completely normal. In this guide, you will find recommendations to help you prepare for and fully enjoy your first pilgrimage, both spiritually and practically.

Define the purpose of your pilgrimage

Every pilgrimage begins with an intention. Before traveling, take a moment to ask yourself what is moving you to begin this journey.

Perhaps you want to give thanks for a blessing, pray for someone, seek guidance for a decision, strengthen your faith, or recover your inner peace. You may also be traveling to learn more about the history of the Church or to experience a deeper encounter with God.

There is no perfect motivation. Every pilgrim begins from a different place and carries a unique story. Writing down your intentions can help you remember them throughout the journey.

Learn about the destination

Learning about the history and meaning of your destination can help you experience the pilgrimage more deeply. Read about the shrines, Marian devotions, saints, or religious events connected to the places you will visit.

You do not need to study extensively; a simple reading can provide the context you need to understand why the destination matters to the Catholic faith.

It is also helpful to review the itinerary provided by the agency so you know:

  • The length of the trip.
  • The expected weather.
  • The level of physical activity.
  • Approximate schedules.
  • Clothing recommendations.
  • Required documents.
  • Entry conditions for the places visited.
  • Services included in the program.

Having this information will help you travel with greater confidence and peace of mind.

Care for your health and participate at your own pace

Rest, stay hydrated, and communicate any discomfort. If you have a medical condition, inform the agency before departure.

You do not need to know every prayer or feel prepared for every activity. Listen, observe, and participate according to your abilities. A pilgrimage is a personal process, and each person may live it at their own pace.

Do you need to be very religious to go on a pilgrimage?

No. Some people begin a pilgrimage with a strong faith, while others go because they are seeking answers, want to give thanks, or need to reconnect with God.

Every pilgrim arrives with a different story. You do not need to compare yourself with others or feel that you must experience the journey in a particular way. What matters is maintaining a respectful and open attitude.

You may begin with doubts, tiredness, or expectations. Each person feels and expresses faith differently. Some pilgrims experience intense emotions, while others live the journey more quietly. Both ways are valid.

Your first pilgrimage can be the beginning of a new path

It is normal to feel nervous before your first pilgrimage, especially if you are traveling alone or do not know the other members of the group. However, a sense of community usually develops from the first days, helping everyone feel accompanied.

To make a pilgrimage is to walk with an intention, discover God's presence in each place, and return with a renewed perspective. The destination matters, but the true transformation takes place in the heart.

At Sky Travel J&M, we want first-time pilgrims to feel guided, accompanied, and free to focus on what matters most: living a true encounter with God.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience to go on a Catholic pilgrimage?
No. You do not need previous experience, knowledge of every prayer, or advanced religious training. An open and respectful attitude is enough to begin.
How can I prepare for my first pilgrimage?
Define your intention, learn about the destination and itinerary, prepare your documents, care for your health, and open your heart to the journey with patience.
Do I need to be very religious to go on a pilgrimage?
No. Each person arrives with a different story and motivation. What matters is maintaining a respectful, open attitude and being willing to live the journey.
What should I review before traveling?
Review the trip duration, expected weather, physical activity level, schedules, clothing recommendations, required documents, entry conditions, and included services.
