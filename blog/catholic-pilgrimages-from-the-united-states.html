What are the most famous pilgrimages?

Among the best-known Catholic pilgrimage destinations are the Holy Land, where Jesus lived; Fátima and Lourdes, two great Marian shrines in Europe; the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe in Mexico; and Medjugorje, which welcomes pilgrims from all over the world. All of them are on our 2027 calendar: Holy Land, Fátima and Lourdes, Guadalupe and Medjugorje.

How long does a pilgrimage last?

It depends on the destination. In 2027, the shortest is Medjugorje, at just over a week, and the longest is the Holy Land with Medjugorje, at almost three weeks. Each program details its day-by-day itinerary.
